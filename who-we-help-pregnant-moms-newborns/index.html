Pregnant Moms & Newborns

Pregnant Moms and Newborns in Las Vegas, NV

The Gonstead Technique has become something of a modern-day birthing phenomenon. Expecting moms are seeking a safer, easier, and more comfortable experience during pregnancy and natural birth. Evergrowth Chiropractic is your certified local chiropractor offering the Website Technique, helping during pregnancy and beyond in Las Vegas, NV. Read on to learn more about the powerful benefits of proper chiropractic care during pregnancy.

What is the Webster Technique?

Birthing coaches, doulas, and medical professionals agree that a natural birth is best for the baby. Those further subscribed to ideal conditions for birthing and baby is the Gonstead Technique. This level of chiropractic care has proven vital to normal physiological function and employs a host of benefits. Those certified in this technique are specially trained in sacral analysis and diversified adjustment.

Sacral Subluxation Not Just For Pregnancy

The dysfunction of the SI joint can happen to anyone, male or female, at any age. While most often occurring during pregnancy these subluxations happen when the sacrum moves slightly beyond its maximum range. That could be an injury as the result of an accident, overextension, poor posture, or arthritis. Regardless of the reason why you are experiencing sacral subluxation or SI joint dysfunction, Evergrowth Chiropractic can help.

What to Expect in Las Vegas, NV

There was never a book written on what to expect when you experience sacral subluxation, and finding help may be more challenging than you think. Many traditional chiropractors aren't familiar with or trained in treating pregnant women. In pregnant women, sacral subluxation could mean a more challenging birth. For this reason, it is recommended that pregnant women see a Website Technique certified provider if they suspect they have developed an issue.

Chiropractic Care For Newborns

Imagine being locked into the fetal position for the better part of a year and finally being able to stretch out. One thing their new environment allows for is GI tract movement. Many babies experience some level of gas, and those extreme cases like colic can be a nightmare. Your baby deserves the very best and chiropractic care can be just what the doctor ordered, for mom and the baby. There's no adjustment so to speak, but more the gentle, guiding hand or the chiropractor at key points to help the body perform at top function.

The Gonstead Technique has been attributed to reducing and eliminating the symptoms associated with some of the common ailments inflicting newborns.

  • Colic
  • Sleeplessness
  • Lack of Bowel Movement
  • Earache/Toothache

You have questions, we have answers. Speak to a Gonstead Technique-certified chiropractor at your next appointment.
