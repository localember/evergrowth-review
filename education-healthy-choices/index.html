Education For Healthy Choices

Education For Healthy Choices in Las Vegas, NV

At Evergrowth Chiropractic we have an education-first motto that has led to us rehabilitating patients in Las Vegas NV, rather than medicating them indefinitely. In our practice, we lean heavily on treatment and recovery, as opposed to medication and surgery. Through proper education regarding our patient's condition, outcome probability, and potential therapies, our patients are able to make the healthiest choices for their future.

Alternative Therapies In Las Vegas, NV

Considering that spinal manipulation and therapies have been developed and used over thousands of years it is nothing short of amazing that chiropractic medicine continues to be considered an alternative. It has, and still is, considered the staple in spinal health, and all the wellness that comes with it. In fact, it is the alternative to chiropractics, meaning medications and surgery, which continues to trap people in a perpetual state of pain and poor health. With our all-encompassing care, you can be sure to receive education from your doctor regarding lifestyle, diet, and nutritional, as well as therapies to make the right choices for your care.

Education Topics

The education topics chosen to specifically treat or care for your condition and ongoing health will be custom-tailored to your needs. If you have specific topics of concern, speak directly with your doctor. Here are some of the more common topics discussed regarding patient care.

Treatments:

  • Therapeutic Exercise
  • Therapeutic Stretches
  • Soft Tissue Manual Therapy
  • Physical Therapy
  • Ultrasound Therapy
  • Ice & Heat Therapy
  • Lifestyle Modifications

Lifestyle Topics:

  • Lifestyle Modification Counseling
  • Diet & Nutritional Counseling
  • Stress Management
  • Improper Lifting
  • Poor Posture

A Pain-Free Life Is Waiting

Most of our patients come to our clinic in pain, hoping to live happier, healthier, and pain-free lives. Together, we create a path to the pain-free life you want. With our assistance, we have helped patients reduce or even stop medications, and increase their quality of life. Our experienced doctors of chiropractic are here to help.
