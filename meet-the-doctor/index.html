Dr. Willie So

Gonstead Chiropractor

Dr. Willie So was born and raised in Las Vegas, NV. He graduated from the University of Nevada, Las Vegas (UNLV) with a Bachelor of Science in Kinesiology. Dr. Willie So went further on to attain a Doctorate of Chiropractic at Life Chiropractic West. His Mission in life is to help his community through specific chiropractic care. Dr. Willie So attributes the foundation of Evergrowth Chiropractic through his painful experience as a patient in the traditional health care system.

After experiencing amazing results through Gonstead chiropractic care, he finally found his calling in life. He attained certainty in his ability in helping people heal naturally by focusing on the spine and nervous system. Dr. Willie So is extremely passionate about getting his community to a healthier life through natural means. Leading with excellence, Dr. Willie So saw the opportunity to open his office, advancing his vision of aiding the people in the community.

Dr. So's Journey to Chiropractic

In the midst of my teenage years, life took an unexpected turn when I was diagnosed with an arteriovenous malformation (AVM)—a complex tangle of blood vessels in my brain. It was a silent threat, lurking unnoticed until a sudden rupture nearly stole my life. The ensuing battle for survival was grueling, marked by surgeries, uncertain recoveries, and a profound awakening to the fragility of life.

Through sheer determination and the unwavering support of my loved ones, I defied the odds and emerged from this harrowing experience with a newfound purpose: to help others navigate their own paths to health and healing. This journey led me to discover the Gonstead method—an advanced chiropractic approach renowned for its precision and effectiveness in addressing spinal misalignments and promoting optimal nerve function.

Driven by my personal experience of overcoming physical challenges, I immersed myself in mastering the Gonstead method. Its emphasis on precise diagnostics and tailored treatments resonated deeply with my journey towards recovery and resilience. Armed with this knowledge, I embarked on a mission to provide compassionate care and holistic healing to my patients.

As I immersed myself in the study and practice of Gonstead chiropractic, I witnessed firsthand its transformative power. The method's emphasis on thorough assessment, meticulous adjustments, and personalized care resonated deeply with my own journey of recovery and resilience. I knew this was my calling—to blend scientific precision with compassionate care, guiding others toward a life free from pain and limitation.

Establishing my Gonstead chiropractic practice became not just a career choice, but a mission to empower individuals to reclaim their health and vitality. I've faced challenges along the way—skepticism about chiropractic care, logistical hurdles in setting up a practice, and the ongoing commitment to continuous learning and improvement. Each challenge strengthened my resolve and deepened my commitment to providing exceptional care to my patients.

Today, as a Gonstead chiropractor, I see my journey mirrored in the lives of those I treat—a journey from vulnerability to strength, from pain to healing. My practice is not just a career, but a testament to the transformative power of chiropractic care. It's about empowering individuals to live their fullest lives, free from limitations imposed by injury or discomfort.

Through community engagement, education, and personalized care, my practice strives to enrich the lives of those we serve. Every adjustment, every consultation, is a step towards restoring health and vitality.
