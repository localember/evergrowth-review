Accessibility Statement

UPDATED: February 2024

General

Evergrowth Chiropractic strives to ensure that its services are accessible to people with disabilities. Evergrowth Chiropractic has invested a significant amount of resources to help ensure that its website is made easier to use and more accessible for people with disabilities, with the strong belief that every person has the right to live with dignity, equality, comfort, and independence.

Accessibility

Consistent with this goal, we have taken several steps to enhance our site and increase its usability by those who access the web using assistive technology. Our efforts are guided by the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, version 2.1, Level AA (WCAG 2.1 AA). We will continue to enhance our digital properties to deliver accessible experiences.

Disclaimer

Evergrowth Chiropractic continues its efforts to constantly improve the accessibility of its site and services in the belief that it is our collective moral obligation to allow seamless, accessible, and unhindered use also for those of us with disabilities.

Despite our efforts to make all pages and content on evergrowthchiro.com fully accessible, some content may not have yet been fully adapted to the strictest accessibility standards. This may be a result of not having found or identified the most appropriate technological solution.

Here For You

If you are experiencing difficulty with any content on evergrowthchiro.com or require assistance with any part of our site, please contact us during normal business hours at (702) 389-5009, and we will be happy to assist.

Contact Us

If you wish to report an accessibility issue, have any questions, or need assistance, please contact Evergrowth Chiropractic at (702) 389-5009.
