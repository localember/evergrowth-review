Athletes & Entertainers

Athletes and Entertainers in Las Vegas, NV

Las Vegas is the land of entertainment and with the addition of major sports teams, we are becoming a destination for professional sporting events, too. Our city historically has been home to training camps, athletes, and athletic entertainers, and amateur sports on every level. What was once considered a trade secret to professional athletes, chiropractic care in Las Vegas NV has hit the mainstream. Today, at Evergrowth Chiropractic, we treat athletes from pediatrics through geriatrics, and every level in between.

Chiropractic Treatment To Prevent Injury In Las Vegas, NV

Athletes are highly educated and knowledgeable about taking care of their bodies. In the same way that they monitor their diet strictly, they monitor and maintain their physical body. When people think about athletes conditioning themselves for optimal performance they think about stretching, strengthening, and endurance building. The part they don't see is how this type of constant working of the bones and muscles, if not performed in the proper position, can cause pain, injury, and even long-term damage.

The team of chiropractic doctors at Evergrowth Chiropractics are specialty trained and highly experienced at providing preventative and recovery care for athletes. Using the Gonstead Technique we are able to gently and effectively position the athlete's soft tissue back into alignment. Ultimately, this results in peak performance.

Peak Performance

Athletes and entertainers don't just want their bodies in great condition, their livelihood depends on it. Helping them to achieve peak performance is Evergrowth Chiropractic. After the first visit, a short evaluation, adjustment, and therapy can realign your posture, allowing you to always be in a state of peak performance.

Muscular Sprains and Tendon or Ligament Strains

Muscular sprains and tendon or ligament strains typically occur when you engage in activities your body isn't accustomed to or experience accidents. Actions like lifting while twisting or stretching beyond your limits can lead to these painful conditions, often accompanied by swelling and bruising in the affected area. Such incidents can also alter the spine's alignment. Fortunately, back strains and sprains tend to respond well to chiropractic care.

Best Athletic Adjustment

For athletes and entertainers, in order to be ready before showtime it is best to have a chiropractic adjustment before you warm up. Luckily, the Evergrowth Chiropractic office is conveniently located in the Las Vegas area, making it easier than ever to stay in optimal condition to maintain your peak performance.
